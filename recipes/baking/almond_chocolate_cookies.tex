\title{Almond Chocolate Sandwiches}
\maketitle
\subtitle{1 hour 15 minutes plus a half hour for chilling}

\noindent \desc{These are a variation on an almond cookie and an oat-chocolate sandwich type cookie. The latter is from Nicole Accetola's ``Scandinavian from scratch''. 
These are a little more bready than an almond cookie might otherwise be, but has more interesting flavors than the oat and chocolate cookie in my opinion. These are meant to be cookie-cutter cookies but you can just as well from little balls and smoosh them into circular enough shapes before baking.}

\recipe{
\begin{ingredients}
  \item 2 sticks unsalted butter
  \item $\frac{1}{2}$ cup granulated sugar
  \item 2 $\frac{1}{2}$ cups nuts
  \item 1 egg
  \item 1 tsp almond extract
  \item zest from a lemon
  \item 1 tbsp cardamom
  \item 1 tsp nutmeg
  \item one and a half cup of all-purpose flour
  \item $\frac{1}{2}$ tsp salt
  \item $\frac{1}{3}$ cup chocolate chips
  \item 1 tbsp milk
\end{ingredients}
}{
\begin{directions}
  \item Blend the nuts finely until they are almost a flour but still have chunks of nut in them. Cream butter with this meal and sugar. Add egg, almond extract, and lemon zest. Set aside.
  \item Mix all dry ingredients and slowly incorporate into the wet. Once fully incorporated set covered in the fridge for a half hour.
  \item Once the half hour is up, set the oven to heat to 350. Roll out the dough on a cutting board and cut round shapes onto a baking sheet.*
  \item Bake for 10 minutes, or until visibly browning, set to cool.
  \item Once all cookies have baked, set the chocolate and milk to melt over a double boiler. Once all melted finish the cookies by painting the cooked side of one cookie with chocolate and closing with another. Let set for 30 minutes.
  \note{Instead of using cookie cutters, you can take the dough from the fridge and form balls, smooshing them with your hands. Try to be consistent about the size of the balls, I try to do one-inch diameter balls. The cooking time may increase if you do this.}
\end{directions}
}
