\title{Strawberry-Almond Tart}
\maketitle
\subtitle{Some amount of time. I don't know right now}

\noindent \desc{Perhaps I have a tendency to call things tarts when they really aren't, but here we are. This recipe was originally taken from Nichole Accetola's \href{https://www.kantinesf.com/cookbook-scandinavian-from-scratch}{Scandinavian from Scratch} cookbook. There it is a strawberry almond tart with a sort of custard topping, this was very good. I've made a few modifications with respect to changing the crust, some proportions, and adding a few bits here and there.  It can be kept with something closer to the listed topping, I prefer whipped cream instead, or for a richer desert, this can be substituted with chocolate quite easily.}

\recipe{
\textbf{Almond Crust:}
\begin{ingredients}
\item 2 $\frac{1}{2}$ cups almond flour 
\item $\frac{1}{2}$ tsp salt 
\item 1 egg 
\item 3 Tbsp coconut oil 
\item 2 Tbsp maple syrup
\item 1 tsp almond extract
\end{ingredients}
\textbf{Almond Filling}
\begin{ingredients}
\item $\frac{1}{4}$ cup almond flour
\item 1 tsp almond extract
\item $\frac{1}{2}$ cup sugar
\item 2 eggs and the white of another
\item Pinch of salt
\item 4 tbsp unsalted butter
\item zest of lemon
\end{ingredients}
\textbf{Topping}
\begin{ingredients}
\item 3 oz bittersweet chocolate
\item $\frac{1}{2}$ cup heavy cream
\item 1 tbsp sugar
\item 2 tsp vanilla extract
\item 2 lbs strawberries, hulled
\item 2 tbsp citrus jam 
\end{ingredients}
}{
\begin{directions}
\item[1.] Preheat oven to 350. Combine all wet ingredients together in a bowl and mold the wet dough into the form of a pie crust in a pie plate or spring-form pan. Bake for 20 minutes or  until outside is crispy.
\item[2.] Meanwhile, mix almond flour, almond extract, sugar, and egg white in a bowl until combined. Add butter and lemon zest and whip until light. Add remaining two eggs and mix until homogeneous. 
\item[3.] Add almond filling to crust, bake until golden, around 25 minutes. While warm spread chocolate on top and let cool.
\item[4.] While pie cools, make the whipped cream by whipping the heavy cream with sugar and vanilla. 
\item[5.] After pie has cooled, top with the cream and strawberries, brushing the tops of strawberries with the jam mixed with 2 tbsp water.
  \note{The whipped cream topping can be replaced with a light chocolate mousse. Make sure not to overdo the chocolate and bury the strawberries though.}
\end{directions}
}
