\title{Black Beans}
\maketitle
\subtitle{Just under 2 hours}

\noindent \desc{A little description}

\recipe{
\begin{ingredients}
  \item 1 large onion
  \item 1 bell pepper
  \item a handful of mushrooms
  \item 3 cloves garlic
  \item 32 oz black beans
  \item 1 tbsp cumin
  \item 1 tbsp coriander
  \item salt to taste
\end{ingredients}
}{
\begin{directions}
  \item Finely chop the onions and cook, salted, on medium heat. While that goes, start cutting up the bell pepper and mushrooms, adding as you finish cutting them. Cook for a half hour or until everything thoroughly looses shape.
  \item Chop up the garlic into small pieces and cook with a little more olive oil until browning. Add into the rest of the veggies.
  \item Drain and rinse the black beans well. Add to the veggies. In the meantime, if you have seeded spices, grind them up and add them. Otherwise, just add them.
  \item Cook for an hour or until the beans are dry, add more oil if necessary, but some sticking is normal. Salt to taste and serve.
\end{directions}
}
